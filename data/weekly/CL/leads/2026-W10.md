# Reviewer leads for W10 (13–19 Nov 2026)

Leads from the reviewer (Cristian, 16 Nov). The weekly run checks each one, codes what it finds, and says in the summary what it could not confirm. Polls already entered in `2026-W10.json` (this folder) are shown on the site before the weekly run; do not enter them twice.

1. **Approval collapse — confirmed.** Agenda Criteria, 15 Nov (fieldwork 12–13 Nov, n=802): approval 18% (−6), disapproval 72% (+5), the lowest of the term. Economic expectations worsened: 60% expect the country's economy to be worse in six months (+11 since October), 46% their household's (+13), 53% expect worse employment (+7). Source: https://www.criteria.cl/site/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Agenda_Criteria_15_Noviembre_2026_11vRs.pdf. Code as government approval and national mood (politics stream, cross-cutting) and consider lowering politics levels where the executive drives the window. Also check Cadem 15 Nov.
2. **Truckers' strike: settled, but contested.** The national strike began 10 Nov (demands: lower fuel prices, specific fuel tax, TAG and tolls). Criteria 15 Nov: 97% heard of it; 80% agree with the mobilisation (72% even on the right); 88% consider the demands justified. The government withdrew its $20 fuel-cap bill on 11 Nov to adjust it: check whether and when it was re-filed and with what changes. Find the agreement, what it gives (fuel, tax, tolls), who signed and who did not, and the criticism that it favours some groups or is not equitable. Topics: T21 fuel prices (organised forces; national mood supports the demands), T16 tolls (possible reopening).
3. **Constitutional accusation against Justice Minister Rabat.** Criteria 15 Nov: 79% disagree with keeping Rabat (+7 since 1 Nov); among those aware of the accusation, 68% agree the opposition should file it (Grau 39% in June 2026); 72% agree he could be removed. Check whether it was filed, the chapters, the vote count (78 of 155 needed; opposition about 64) and the positions of PDG, independents and UDI. Topic: T04 (politics: national mood strongly supports the accusation). Also from 11 Nov (El Mostrador): Rabat handed over 308 sentence-reduction decrees after Senate pressure, and a report on five Supreme Court rulings he did not mention to Congress.

Also: the Unholster DataDriven newsletter (14 Nov) reported 32 truckers' strikes since 1990, with diesel rising before 24 of the 29 with known dates. Use as a precedent for T21, not as a weekly item.
