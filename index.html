Policy windows in Chile, week by week

Policies tend to change when three things line up: a problem people see as urgent, political conditions that allow action, and a solution ready to use (Kingdon's multiple streams). Every week this monitor reads Chile's news, political programmes, polls, indicators and Congress, and rates the first two for each topic on the agenda. When both are strong at the same time, a policy window may be open: a short period when change is more likely.

  1. Problem streamIs the issue seen as a problem? Events, data, reports, public attention. Rated 0 to 3.
  2. Politics streamCan politics act on it? Government plans, votes, approval, organised pressure. Rated 0 to 3.
  3. Potential windowBoth streams at 2 or 3 in the same week.

Decision points ahead: when could a window be used or close?
